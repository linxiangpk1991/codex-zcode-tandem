## Original art direction

Create a beautiful wide 2:1 landscape hero cover for an independent open-source developer project named "ZCode Tandem". Editorial product illustration, premium contemporary Swiss design, warm ivory paper background with extremely subtle grain. Large elegant near-black sans-serif exact title "ZCode Tandem" in the upper left, smaller exact line below "Two agents. One direction.". In the lower-right and center, a sculptural abstract tandem mechanism: two rounded architectural loop forms, one deep forest teal and one warm terracotta, linked along a single thin sweeping graphite track leading forward. The pair are distinct yet balanced, conveying thoughtful collaboration and deliberate motion, soft realistic studio light, precise shadows, tactile matte ceramic and lightly brushed metal, restrained tiny warm orange connector. Generous negative space and intentional asymmetry, beautifully art-directed composition, readable even in a GitHub README thumbnail. A tiny geometric paired-loop symbol may accompany the title. Keep everything uncluttered. No people, no robots, no brains, no official product logos, no glowing circuits, no charts, no fake software screenshots, no purple gradients, no extra text or watermarks. Render a finished professional brand cover, 2000 by 1000 composition.
